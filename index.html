About

How I work

I start from a real need, not a brief — then build just enough to find out if the idea actually holds up when someone uses it.

My background moves across graphic design, service design, market intelligence and founding my own ventures — which is exactly the mix behind this positioning: Product & Service Design informed by behavioral science and learning design, applied to how people and organizations navigate an AI-driven world of work.

Every case study on this site was designed and prototyped working directly with AI as a build partner — from research and flows through to a clickable product. It's both a working method and part of the point I'm making.
