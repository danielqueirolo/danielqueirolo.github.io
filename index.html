From a hunch to a shipped product

This is the whole loop I run, shown with real moments from building Embarkei.

  1. Discover

    Noticed new designers at a multinational onboarding alone, then heard the same story at other companies.

    Observation, desk research
  2. Research

    Talked to leadership, People & Culture and the designers who had lived through it.

    Interviews, synthesis
  3. Prototype

    Paper first, then Figma: a task flow for the new hire, a Buddy, a two-week retrospective.

    Figma, user flows
  4. Build with AI

    Turned the design into a multi-company product, with roles, journeys and private logbooks.

    Claude, Next.js, Supabase
  5. Test

    24 automated checks for data isolation between companies. WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode.

    Accessibility, security tests
  6. Ship

    Deployed and live, with privacy tools to export, correct and delete personal data.

    Vercel, LGPD
  7. Market

    Designed and wrote the sales site and invitation emails. Now preparing pilots with first companies.

    Landing page, email, pilots
